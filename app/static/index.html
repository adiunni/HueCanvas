With audio listening active, white lamps follow music in all lighting modes through smooth brightness changes every few beats. They do not support fast Entertainment strobes. Brightness-only mode preserves every bulb’s current color.

Bass takes priority. Mids trigger only below the bass fallback level. Crossings create pulses; each band rearms after falling 2 dB below its trigger. The See the sound cards show comparable band RMS levels. These thresholds depend on playback volume.

Increase the delay if the lights lead the audible beat. Before a tempo estimate is ready, palette blending uses the Animation cycle speed. Percussion triggers react to actual attacks; drop accents estimate an energy lift and may miss or misidentify drops.

Silence while listening restores the pre-Start state; music resumes the effect automatically. Stop holds the current look. Restore ends playback and returns the pre-Start state.

Allow audio-input access when macOS asks. BPM needs 8–20 seconds. Percussion triggers work without waiting for a BPM estimate. Gentle and sharp pulses both support Together and Staggered patterns. Ordinary sharp flashes are limited to three per second; detected rolls can follow attacks up to your roll limit (eight per second by default). Color bulbs pulse on individual attacks; white bulbs follow slow musical groups. Staggered also alternates the white targets once BPM is available.
